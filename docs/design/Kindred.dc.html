The story

Two people, one bolt of canvas, no seasons.

Form & Void started in 2021 when Wies and Tomas bought a roll of deadstock cotton duck from a shuttered sailmaker two streets over. They cut six jackets, sold them to friends, and never wrote a business plan.

Everything is still made in the same room above a bike shop. When a fabric runs out, the piece is gone — there are no restocks and no forecasting spreadsheets.
